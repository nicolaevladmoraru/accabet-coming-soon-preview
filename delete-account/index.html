Delete your AccaBet account

Last updated: 14 November 2026
Request permanent deletion of your AccaBet account and associated personal data. AccaBet is operated by Nicolae Vlad Moraru, a UK sole trader. This page accepts requests through email; it does not automatically delete an account.

1Request deletion

Email accabet.official@gmail.com with the subject “AccaBet account deletion”. Tell us that you want your AccaBet account and associated data deleted. If available, include the non-secret support reference shown in the app's Delete Account explanation. Do not email passwords, installation secrets, identity tokens or payment-card details.

We verify control of the relevant installation or linked account before processing the request. An email address, user ID or installation ID alone is not proof of ownership. We will explain the verification needed; we will not delete someone else's account because an email names it. If you no longer have the original installation, additional ownership checks are needed. Requests are subject to applicable data-protection deadlines and verification requirements.

2Delete inside Android

In an Android version containing this feature, open Settings > Delete Account, read the consequences and explicitly confirm. The app authenticates with its existing installation credentials. Local tickets and recovery archives are cleared only after the backend confirms deletion. If confirmation fails or the network outcome is uncertain, recoverable local data is kept and sync is paused until you retry. If your installed version does not show Delete Account, use the email request above. Reset Personal Data is not complete account deletion.

3Data removed

Verified deletion removes the identity's live saved Accas and selections, fixture-save history, generation-signature history and personal statistics source records; notification preferences, push-delivery registration and delivery history; installation credentials; and any associated Google account link, purchase-verification records and linked VIP personal records. The requesting Android version also clears its account-linked local tickets, history, recovery archives and credentials. Shared football fixtures, match results, prediction-engine data, other users and unrelated display preferences are preserved. A multi-device account requires support verification rather than deletion of another device's data based on one installation alone. Your Google account is not deleted.

4Limited retention and backups

One-way installation and credential revocation digests are kept indefinitely to reject erased credentials and prevent deleted data from being re-uploaded. They do not contain the original installation ID, account identifier, email, Google identifier or ticket content. These are security records, not retained account content.

Protected backups and separate archives are not rewritten by the live deletion endpoint. The existing weekly policy retains the latest four successful weekly generations; manual archives do not automatically expire. This is not a promise of erasure from every backup within four calendar weeks. Backups must remain restricted to recovery; erased identities must not be reactivated when restoring an older backup, and the current revocation register must be preserved. Google may retain billing records under its own policies. No additional statutory retention period is asserted without a specific applicable legal obligation. See our Privacy Policy for details.

5Cancel Google Play separately

Deleting your AccaBet account ends account-linked Premium or VIP access. It does not cancel Google Play billing or automatically issue a refund. Manage or cancel through Google Play subscriptions, using the Google account that purchased the subscription. Read the Terms & Conditions for cancellation and refund information.
