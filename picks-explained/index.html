Below are all picks currently available in AccaBet. Each explanation shows exactly what must happen for the pick to win or lose.

SIX PICKS — REGULATION TIME

Full Time Over 1.5

At least 2 total goals.

Add goals scored by both teams. Regulation time includes stoppage time and excludes extra time and penalty shootouts.

WIN

2-0, 1-1, 1-2

LOSE

0-0, 1-0, 0-1

Full Time Over 2.5

At least 3 total goals.

Add goals scored by both teams. Regulation time includes stoppage time and excludes extra time and penalty shootouts.

WIN

2-1, 3-0, 2-2

LOSE

1-1, 2-0, 0-2

BTTS YES (Both teams to score)

Both teams must score at least 1 goal.

The home team and the away team must both score. Regulation time includes stoppage time and excludes extra time and penalty shootouts.

WIN

1-1, 2-1, 1-3

LOSE

0-0, 1-0, 0-2

Full Time Over 2.5 & BTTS YES

At least 3 total goals AND both teams score.

Both conditions must be met in the same match. Regulation time includes stoppage time and excludes extra time and penalty shootouts.

WIN

2-1, 1-2, 2-2

LOSE

1-1, 3-0, 0-3

Home Team Over 1.5

The home team must score at least 2 goals.

Only the home team goals count, regardless of the away score. Regulation time includes stoppage time and excludes extra time and penalty shootouts.

WIN

2-0, 2-1, 3-4

LOSE

0-2, 1-3, 1-0

Away Team Over 1.5

The away team must score at least 2 goals.

Only the away team goals count, regardless of the home score. Regulation time includes stoppage time and excludes extra time and penalty shootouts.

WIN

0-2, 1-2, 3-4

LOSE

2-0, 3-1, 0-1
