Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about football picks, accumulator tickets and the results you see on AccaBet.

What is AccaBet?

AccaBet is a football analysis and accumulator-generation service. The website lets you explore published picks, ticket results, upcoming fixtures and historical pick statistics. AccaBet is not a bookmaker and does not accept bets or hold betting funds.

Are football predictions guaranteed?

No. Predictions and displayed probabilities are estimates based on the football data available to AccaBet. Unexpected events, incomplete information and changes in a match can affect any selection. A high probability or past Win Rate does not guarantee a future result.

Which pick categories does AccaBet cover?

The six categories are Full Time Over 1.5, Full Time Over 2.5, BTTS YES, Full Time Over 2.5 & BTTS YES, Home Team Over 1.5 and Away Team Over 1.5. Availability varies by market and fixture. These markets use regulation time, including stoppage time, rather than extra time or penalty shootouts. Visit Picks Explained for the rules and examples.

How does an ACCA combination work?

An accumulator combines multiple selections into one ticket. Its success depends on the included selections: one lost selection makes the accumulator lose. Pending and void selections are handled separately. Adding more selections increases the number of conditions that must succeed; it does not guarantee a better outcome.

AccaBet displays tickets for information. If you choose to bet with a third-party bookmaker, that bookmaker's prices and settlement rules apply.

Can I customise a ticket?

The Android app's builder provides options for the match day, leagues, selection count, supported markets, odds range, minimum estimated success percentage and target odds. It needs eligible fixtures and selections to match your settings, so a particular combination may not be available. The website currently displays published tickets; the builder is an Android app feature.

Are the website's published tickets free to view?

Yes. You can view the tickets shown on Today's Accas without a subscription. Tickets appear when published, and availability varies by date. This does not mean every market or ticket size will be available each day.

How is Win Rate calculated?

Win Rate = WIN ÷ (WIN + LOST) × 100. Only confirmed WIN and LOST Main Picks from completed matches are counted. The settled total is the sum of those two counts. Pending, postponed, cancelled, void and unconfirmed results do not enter the calculation.

Each match and market combination counts once. This is a historical pick statistic, not a predicted chance of winning an accumulator or a measure of profit.

Which dates do the statistics filters use?

Statistics use match kickoff dates in UK time (GMT / BST). The 7-day and 30-day filters include today. The 3-month, 6-month and 12-month filters start on the corresponding calendar date that many months ago, or the last valid day of that month. All Time includes all available recorded history.

Why do I see a dash or different totals on different pages?

A dash in Win Rate means there are no eligible settled results for that market and period. AccaBet does not fill missing history with estimated results. The Statistics page counts individual Main Picks, while Results shows published tickets that may contain several selections. Their totals are not expected to be identical.

Why can a selection show WIN while the match is still live?

Some conditions become irreversible before full time. For example, an Over 1.5 selection can be confirmed after the second goal. The match may still be live, and other selections on the ticket may remain pending. Historical Statistics waits for a confirmed completed match before counting that selection.

Why is a result pending or a final score missing?

A result stays pending when confirmation is not yet available. Match information can be delayed or incomplete, and fixtures may be postponed, cancelled or otherwise unresolved. A final score is shown only when verified; AccaBet does not invent an outcome to complete a ticket.

How often does the website update?

While their pages are visible, Statistics and Match Centre refresh automatically every 60 seconds. Published tickets and Results refresh every 90 seconds. Updates depend on new information becoming available; these intervals do not guarantee instant confirmation.

If a connection fails, the page shows a temporary error and retries automatically. Where the last successful update remains visible, it is labelled accordingly.
