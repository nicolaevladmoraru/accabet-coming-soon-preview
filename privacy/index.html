Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026
Effective date: 14 November 2026. This Privacy Policy explains how Nicolae Vlad Moraru, trading as AccaBet and acting as the data controller, handles information when you use the AccaBet Android application or the AccaBet website at www.accabet.co.uk. AccaBet is a football accumulator generation and analysis service operated from the United Kingdom. AccaBet does not accept wagers, hold betting balances, process gambling stakes or act as a bookmaker.

1Information stored on your device

AccaBet may store application preferences and user-created data locally on your device. This can include odds format, notification preferences, privacy settings, saved Acca tickets, selected markets, generated selections and cached match or selection results. Local application data remains on the device unless a feature specifically requires communication with an AccaBet service.

The website also stores display preferences, such as odds format and match sorting, in browser local storage. Website analytics cookies and consent choices are described separately below. Saved tickets are not necessarily device-only: installation-linked services, notifications and optional account synchronisation can require the server records described below.

2Service and technical data

When the application communicates with AccaBet services, standard technical information may be processed, including IP address, request metadata, application requests and information needed to provide, secure and troubleshoot the service. AccaBet may also use an installation identifier and related security information where required to provide notification features and protect the service against abuse.

Where Google Sign-In and account features are enabled, choosing Google Sign-In sends a Google-issued identity token to the AccaBet backend for verification. The backend uses the verified Google account identifier (the token subject) and links it to an AccaBet user identifier. Linking an existing installation also uses its installation identifier and authentication secret. The account-linking records use identifiers rather than your Google password; the current account-linking implementation does not store Google profile names or email addresses as account identity. The identity token is used for verification rather than persisted in application preferences.

When enabled and used, account recovery and saved-ticket synchronisation process ticket identifiers, match selections, odds, fixture identifiers and deletion markers linked to the AccaBet account. Separate installation-linked generation and performance records may also be held on the server. These records support saved-ticket recovery, duplicate prevention and performance features; they are distinct from the visible local ticket list.

For Google Play subscription verification, the application sends a purchase token to the AccaBet backend. The backend checks it with Google Play and stores the purchase token and its hash, the associated AccaBet user identifier, subscription state, expiry and verification information. An obfuscated account identifier binds a purchase to its account. Google handles billing and payment details; the AccaBet purchase-verification flow does not receive your full payment-card details. These optional account and billing features process data only when available and used; their description does not mean that every installation has them enabled.

3Football and fixture data

AccaBet obtains and processes football information required to provide its features. This may include fixtures, leagues, teams, match status, scores, statistics, team logos, markets and other football-related information used to generate or evaluate selections.

4Global statistics

AccaBet uses aggregated application-wide performance information from its prediction engine to calculate Global Stats. These statistics measure the performance of generated Main Picks across markets, probability ranges, leagues and other analytical categories. Global Stats are based on the performance of the AccaBet engine and are separate from a user's saved Accas and My Stats.

5Notifications

If result notifications are enabled, AccaBet may process saved matches and selections to determine whether a selection or Acca is won, lost or unresolved. A selection result may be identified before full time when the selected market has become mathematically irreversible. For example, an Over selection may be marked WIN once the required number of goals has already been scored. The underlying match can remain live until its normal completion. AccaBet provides result-based notifications and is not designed to provide goal, minute-by-minute or live-score alert notifications.

Notification delivery uses Firebase Cloud Messaging. The backend may store a push-delivery token, installation identifier, authentication information, notification preferences and saved-ticket selections needed to identify result events. Event and delivery records help prevent duplicate alerts and diagnose delivery failures. Notification permission and preferences control delivery; disabling alerts is not the same as deleting account or historical service records.

6How information is used

Information is used where necessary to provide AccaBet features, remember preferences, save and evaluate Accas, deliver requested result notifications, calculate performance statistics, maintain reliability, diagnose technical problems, prevent abuse and protect the service.

Where UK data protection law applies, processing necessary to provide requested app, account, saved-ticket and subscription services is based on performance of a contract or steps requested before entering one. Optional website analytics uses consent, which can be withdrawn through Cookie settings. Service security, fraud and abuse prevention, reliability and proportionate troubleshooting rely on legitimate interests in protecting users and operating the service. Processing necessary to comply with applicable legal duties relies on legal obligation. Optional notification delivery is requested and controlled through app and Android settings; Android permission is required where applicable. Information necessary for authentication, purchase verification or a requested feature must be provided to use that feature; declining optional analytics does not block website access.

7Service providers

AccaBet does not sell personal information. AccaBet may use third-party infrastructure, football-data and platform services where necessary to operate the application. These services may process limited technical information required to deliver their functions. AccaBet currently uses Railway for backend infrastructure, Firebase Cloud Messaging for push-notification delivery, Google services associated with Android distribution and application operation, and external football-data services used to provide football information.

Google services include Google Sign-In for authentication, Google Play for billing and purchase verification, Firebase Cloud Messaging for notification delivery and, subject to website consent, Google Analytics 4. These providers may act as processors or independent controllers for their own services, depending on their role. Google billing and account data are also subject to Google's Privacy Policy. Railway hosts the backend. Website hosting and external football-data or image services may receive technical connection data when serving requests; supplying football data does not by itself require sending a user's saved-ticket history to a football-data provider.

These services may involve processing outside the United Kingdom. Applicable transfer requirements depend on the provider, destination and processing activity. Information about the relevant processing locations and transfer arrangements can be requested at accabet.official@gmail.com; this policy does not assert a particular hosting region or transfer mechanism that has not been verified.

The public website is served through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection and security information when hosting the website; see GitHub's privacy statement. Public football images are also requested from API-Sports and Flagcdn; those providers receive the technical information inherent in the browser request.

8Data retention

Locally saved Acca history is retained on the device as part of the user performance history. The in-app Reset Personal Data option in Settings removes saved tickets from the active local list. It is not complete account or personal-data erasure: the implementation retains recovery archives, ticket-deletion markers and generation or performance history. Deleting a visible ticket does not necessarily erase its underlying historical records. App preferences and notification settings are not removed by this action. Local application data may also be removed by clearing application storage, uninstalling the application or through operating-system data management. Server-side operational records are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant feature, security, reliability or legal requirement.

Account links and synchronised tickets are retained to support account access and recovery while those services are used. Purchase records support entitlement checks, restoration, disputes and applicable legal obligations. Installation-linked selection history and deletion markers support statistics, duplicate prevention and preventing deleted tickets from reappearing. Notification records support delivery and deduplication; technical records support security and troubleshooting. Retention depends on the continuing need for each purpose and any applicable legal requirement; no fixed period or automatic account-erasure schedule is promised. Google may retain its own billing records under its policies. Uninstalling the app does not erase server records or cancel a Google Play subscription.

9Your controls

You can control result notifications, notification sound, vibration and available privacy or statistics preferences from Settings. Android system settings may also be used to control application permissions and notifications. Where applicable, subscription controls are provided through Google Play.

Subject to applicable law and the basis for processing, you may request access to your personal data, correction, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability, and may object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may withdraw consent where processing relies on it without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. To exercise these rights or request deletion of account-linked or server-held data, email accabet.official@gmail.com, describing the request and the relevant AccaBet account or installation if known. Do not send passwords, identity tokens or payment-card details. Reasonable identity verification may be required. Requests are subject to applicable legal deadlines, exceptions and retention duties.

The application currently has no dedicated complete-account deletion control, and this website has no automated account-deletion form. Signing out, resetting the visible ticket list or deleting local app storage does not delete the Google account link or all server-held data. The email address above is a contact for requests, not a claim that automated account erasure is implemented. Cancellation of Google Play billing is a separate action. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office or another competent data protection authority.

10Children

AccaBet is intended only for adults aged 18 or over. It is not directed to children and AccaBet does not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children.

11Security

AccaBet uses reasonable technical measures intended to protect application data, service access and communications. No electronic storage, internet service or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when AccaBet features, infrastructure, service providers or legal requirements change. Material changes may be communicated through the application or another appropriate channel. The effective date shown in this policy identifies the current revision.
