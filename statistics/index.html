How these figures are counted

Win Rate = WIN ÷ (WIN + LOST) × 100. Settled selections are the sum of confirmed official wins and losses.

UK kickoff dates determine which period includes a pick. Today, 7 Days and 30 Days include the current UK day. All Time begins on the earliest available official Main Pick date. A dash marks unavailable figures, including VOID counts and percentages that require a VOID count.
