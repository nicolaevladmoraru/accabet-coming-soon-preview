How these figures are counted

Win Rate = WIN ÷ (WIN + LOST) × 100. Each recorded Main Pick counts once per match and market. Only existing WIN or LOST decisions for confirmed completed matches are included.

Periods use match kickoff dates in UK time (GMT / BST). Day filters include today; month filters start on the corresponding date 3, 6 or 12 calendar months ago. All Time includes all available recorded history. Pending, postponed, cancelled, void and unconfirmed results are excluded. A dash means no confirmed results are available for that market and period.
