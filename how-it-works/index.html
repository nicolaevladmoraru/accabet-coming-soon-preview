02 / ODDS RANGE

Choose your odds range

Set minimum and maximum odds to narrow the eligible selections to your preferred price range.

Decimal odds show the total return per unit staked, including the stake. For example, 2.50 represents a total return of ?2.50 for a ?1 stake if successful.

03 / ODDS RANGE

A familiar fractional format

Switch to fractional odds if you prefer prices expressed as potential profit relative to the stake. For example, 3/2 means ?1.50 profit for a ?1 stake if successful, plus the returned stake.

The minimum and maximum controls serve the same purpose in either format.

04 / TARGET ODDS

Aim for combined ticket odds

Choose your desired combined ticket odds in decimal format. AccaBet automatically determines the number of matches to aim for that target using available selections.

Target Odds does not mean a fixed number of selections. The target is the combined odds of the ticket, not the number of matches.

05 / TARGET ODDS

Your target. Your format.

You can also express your desired combined ticket odds in fractional format. AccaBet determines how many matches to use to aim for that target.

Generate Acca creates a ticket based on your selected criteria and the available matches. Suitable selections and an exact target are not guaranteed.

GENERATE YOUR ACCA

Once you've selected your preferences, tap Generate Acca.

AccaBet opens a new screen displaying your generated ticket.

Review the selected football matches, prediction markets, individual odds, estimated probabilities and total combined odds.

Check your selections before saving your ticket.

Estimated probabilities are not guarantees of future results.

SAVE YOUR ACCA

Happy with your generated ticket?

Tap the orange Save Acca button at the bottom of the ticket screen.

Your ticket will be saved in My Accas, where you can access it again and follow its results.

FIND YOUR SAVED ACCAS

Open My Accas from the bottom navigation menu.

Your saved tickets appear here, showing their current status, number of selections, creation date and total combined odds.

Use the available time and status filters to find specific tickets.

You can also search by team name.

VIEW YOUR ACCA DETAILS

Tap any saved ticket to open its detailed view.

See the matches included in your Acca, team names, selected prediction markets, individual odds, estimated probabilities and match statuses.

The ticket header also displays its creation date, current status and total combined odds.
