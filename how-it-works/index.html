Understand the picks, explore accumulator combinations and review the results. AccaBet provides football analysis tools; no prediction is a guaranteed outcome.

How match analysis works

AccaBet uses available football data, including fixtures, team performance and goal statistics, to assess supported markets and identify selections. Match coverage and selection availability depend on the data and markets available for each fixture.

A displayed probability is an estimate for a particular selection. It is not a promise that the selection will win, and it is different from a historical Win Rate. Football matches remain uncertain, even when a selection has a high estimated probability.

The six pick categories

These markets cover regulation time, including stoppage time, and exclude extra time and penalty shootouts.

Full Time Over 1.5
At least two goals must be scored in total across both teams.
Full Time Over 2.5
At least three goals must be scored in total across both teams.
BTTS YES
Both teams must each score at least one goal.
Full Time Over 2.5 & BTTS YES
The match must have at least three goals in total, with both teams scoring.
Home Team Over 1.5
The home team must score at least two goals, regardless of the away team's score.
Away Team Over 1.5
The away team must score at least two goals, regardless of the home team's score.

Market availability can vary. A category listed here does not mean that a qualifying pick or published ticket is currently available. See Picks Explained for winning and losing examples.

Customise your ACCA

An accumulator combines several selections into one ticket. In the AccaBet Android app, the builder lets you choose the match day, league coverage, number of selections and supported football markets. Available filters include an odds range, a minimum estimated success percentage and target total odds.

The builder looks for eligible selections that fit your settings. Availability depends on suitable fixtures and data; a particular combination or target is not guaranteed. Adding selections creates more conditions that must succeed and does not make a ticket safer.

Total odds combine the recorded prices of the selections. Prices may change and can differ between bookmakers. AccaBet creates and displays tickets but does not place bets or accept stakes.

How results are confirmed

AccaBet checks recorded match information to confirm selection outcomes. A selection can be marked WIN before full time when its condition has become irreversible, such as an Over 1.5 pick after the second goal. This does not mean that the match has finished or that every selection on the ticket has won.

Results remain pending when reliable confirmation is unavailable. Postponements, cancellations and void decisions are treated separately from normal wins and losses. The Results page shows published ticket outcomes; final scores are displayed only when verified.

How historical statistics are calculated

The Statistics page reports recorded Main Pick performance for each market. Each match and market combination counts once. Only confirmed WIN or LOST selections from completed matches enter the calculation.

Win Rate = WIN ÷ (WIN + LOST) × 100. The settled total is WIN + LOST. Pending, postponed, cancelled, void and unconfirmed results are excluded. An early WIN enters these statistics only after the match is confirmed as completed.

Choose the last 7 days, 30 days, 3 months, 6 months, 12 months or all available history. Periods use match kickoff dates in UK time (GMT / BST). Statistics refresh every 60 seconds while the page is open. A dash means no eligible settled results are available for that market and period; it is not an estimated percentage.

Historical Win Rate describes past results, not the probability that your next ticket will win. Pick statistics and published ticket results measure different things, so their totals may differ.
